# 🎓 Student Planner App UX Research Case Study
Project Overview
This UX research project explores how college students organize their coursework, assignments, and deadlines. The goal was to understand common organizational challenges and identify opportunities to design a student planner application that simplifies academic planning and improves time management.

## Problem Statement
Many college students rely on multiple tools—such as paper planners, digital calendars, note-taking applications, and learning management systems—to keep track of coursework. Switching between these tools often creates confusion, increases the likelihood of missed deadlines, and makes it difficult to effectively manage academic responsibilities.
This research aimed to better understand students' organizational habits and identify features that could improve their planning experience.

## Research Goals
The objectives of this study were to:
	•	Understand how students currently organize coursework.
	•	Identify frustrations with existing planning methods.
	•	Discover which features students value most in a student planner application.
	•	Identify opportunities to improve the overall user experience.

## Research Questions
	•	How do students currently stay organized?
	•	What frustrations do they experience while managing coursework?
	•	Which features would help students better manage assignments and deadlines?
	•	How could a planner reduce stress and improve productivity?

## Research Methods
Three research methods were used throughout this study.
User Interviews
Five college students participated in one-on-one interviews discussing their study habits, organizational methods, and daily academic routines.
Online Survey
An online survey gathered additional information regarding preferred planning methods, frequently used applications, and desired planner features.
Competitive Analysis
Existing productivity applications were reviewed to understand common features, strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities for improvement.
Applications reviewed included:
	•	Google Calendar
	•	Notion
	•	Todoist
	•	Canvas (Learning Management System)

## User Journey
Current Workflow
	1	Receive a new assignment from an instructor.
	2	Record the assignment in a planner or calendar.
	3	Review assignment details through the learning management system.
	4	Complete the assignment over several days.
	5	Submit the assignment before the deadline.

## Pain Points
	•	Coursework information is spread across multiple applications.
	•	Deadlines are occasionally overlooked.
	•	Existing reminder systems lack flexibility.
	•	Prioritizing assignments can become overwhelming.
	•	Switching between several applications slows productivity.

## Opportunities
	•	Create one centralized assignment dashboard.
	•	Offer customizable reminders and notifications.
	•	Provide calendar synchronization.
	•	Organize assignments using color-coded priorities.
	•	Display weekly and monthly workload summaries.

## Key Findings
The interviews and survey revealed several common themes:
	•	Four out of five participants preferred having all assignments displayed in one location.
	•	Four out of five participants wanted customizable reminders.
	•	Three out of five participants found switching between multiple applications frustrating.
	•	Most participants valued simple and uncluttered interfaces.
	•	Students preferred viewing assignments organized by priority and due date.
	•	Time management became more difficult during weeks with multiple deadlines.

## Design Recommendations
Based on the research findings, the following features are recommended:
	•	Assignment dashboard displaying all upcoming coursework
	•	Color-coded priorities for assignments and exams
	•	Calendar integration with automatic due-date synchronization
	•	Customizable reminders and notifications
	•	Weekly overview of upcoming coursework
	•	Progress tracking for completed assignments
	•	Quick search for courses and assignments
	•	Personalized dashboard with today's tasks and upcoming deadlines
